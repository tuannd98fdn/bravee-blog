## Why I Started This Blog

Every senior developer I admire has one thing in common: **they share what they know**. Whether it's through blog posts, conference talks, or open-source contributions, they give back to the community that helped them grow.

I decided it's time for me to do the same.

<Callout type="tip" title="The learning multiplier">
Writing about what you learn forces you to understand it deeply. You can't explain something you don't truly understand.
</Callout>

## What You'll Find Here

This blog covers my core areas of interest and learning:

### 🤖 AI & Machine Learning
From LLMs and AI Agents to RAG systems and practical applications. I'll break down complex concepts into digestible pieces.

### ☁️ Cloud & AWS
Real-world cloud architecture, AWS services deep-dives, and infrastructure patterns that scale.

### ☕ Java & Backend
Enterprise patterns, Spring Boot best practices, and the art of writing clean, maintainable Java code.

### 🅰️ Angular & Frontend
Component architecture, state management, and building responsive web applications.

### 🏗️ System Design
Distributed systems, microservices, and the architectural decisions that matter.
